External music services
-----------------------
The Musikfinder queries public endpoints of Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive,
ccMixter, Free To Use and Apple/iTunes. Media files, metadata and store offers
remain subject to the terms and licenses of their respective providers. This
project does not grant additional rights to third-party media.


YouTube / YouTube Music
-----------------------
The app does not scrape YouTube, does not call unofficial YouTube proxy/search
APIs and does not download YouTube content. It only creates ordinary user-facing
search URLs from title/artist metadata already obtained from other sources. The
YouTube request happens only after the user activates the external link.
